Refund Policy

Last updated: 2024

1. Initial Booking Amount / Deposit

Due to the custom nature of web development and AI automation, the initial booking fee (e.g., ₹999 or project deposit) is strictly non-refundable. This secures your spot in our development queue and covers initial consultation and architecture planning.

2. Mid-Project Cancellations

If you choose to cancel a project after development has begun, any payments made up to that milestone are non-refundable to cover the time and resources already invested by our team.

3. Final Deliverables

Once a project is completed, approved, and handed over to you, no refunds will be issued. We ensure to address all revisions and bug fixes agreed upon in the project scope before final handover.

4. Dispute Resolution

If you are unsatisfied with our service at any point, please reach out to us directly via WhatsApp or Phone at 08967261996. We strive for 100% client satisfaction and will work with you to resolve any technical issues.
